# Burning Bridges AI

**Motion transfer & object swap for familiar clips**

🌐 Website: [Burning Bridges AI](https://burningbridges.ai/)

## About

Burning Bridges AI is a browser AI video studio for motion transfer, object swap and restyle. The headline flow keeps an original room, camera movement, performance and soundtrack while your photos define a new cast and outfits. A focused “pay per MV” option creates a ~15-second 480p 16:9 clip with the original soundtrack for $9.90 (up to 255 credits reserved per MV), with no subscription required and credits returned on failure.

## Highlights

- Reimagine videos with Burning Bridges AI: combine a source clip with character, outfit, product or scene references for motion transfer and object swap.
- Burning Bridges AI is a video remix studio. Keep a familiar performance’s room, camera, motion and soundtrack, upload cast or object photos, and create a short MV—pay per video from $9.90 or use credits across motion transfer, object swap and restyle tools.
